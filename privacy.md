This is a content site. There are no accounts, no checkout, and no forms, so we never ask for your name, email, or payment details. Here is what does happen when you visit.

## Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand which guides people read and how they found them. Google Analytics sets cookies and collects standard usage data: pages viewed, approximate location (city level), device and browser type, and how you arrived at the site. We use this in aggregate to decide what to write next. You can opt out of Google Analytics entirely with the [Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).

## Advertising cookies and remarketing

We use Google Analytics advertising features, including remarketing. This means third-party vendors, including Google, may use the cookies set on this site to show you ads on other websites and apps based on your past visits here.

You can opt out:

- Adjust or turn off ad personalization in [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com)
- Opt out of many third-party advertising cookies at [aboutads.info/choices](https://www.aboutads.info/choices/)

## What we don't do

- We don't sell or share personal information.
- We don't run comment systems, newsletters, or logins, so we hold no reader accounts or email lists.
- We don't collect precise location data.

## Changes

If our practices change, this page changes with them. The date below is the last revision.
